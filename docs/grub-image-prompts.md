# Grub Card Art Prompts — Filled In (for Gemini)

One filled-in prompt per Grub, generated from the roster in
[`data/grubCards.json`](../data/grubCards.json). Grubs don't level up the way
chickens and predators do — each is a single card, so there's just one image
per Grub, not a 3-stage set. Lower priority than the chicken/predator art, so
these prompts are intentionally lighter — same overall style, no per-item
"posture and condition" staging.

Two entries (**Four Leaf Clover**, **Wild Grain**) aren't animals — they're
rendered as small plants/objects instead of critters. Everything else is a
garden-variety bug or small creature, not a threat — keep these charming and
naturalistic, not menacing (that's the predators' job).

Shared style notes (already baked into every prompt below): painterly
gouache/watercolor, Everdell/Cascadia-style, warm natural lighting, muted warm
palette, simple low-detail background vignette (garden/underbrush), no
text/numbers/icons/UI chrome, 3:4 portrait aspect ratio, high-resolution
print-quality with even margin for card-frame cropping.

Suggested filenames once these are generated: `ui/assets/grubs/<slug>.jpg`
(e.g. `scorpion.jpg`, `four-leaf-clover.jpg`), matching the pattern
`ui/src/predatorArt.js` already uses for predators — one file per Grub, no
season/stage suffix needed.

## 1. Scorpion

```
Generate a painterly gouache/watercolor illustration of a Scorpion, a small
garden critter card for a cozy farming-village board game (style reference:
Everdell, Cascadia) — naturalistic and a little wary, not menacing.

Composition:
- Full-body, three-quarter view, facing right, tail arched up over the back
- Segmented tan-brown body, curved venomous tail arched over the back, small
  pincers, eight legs
- Soft warm natural lighting, gentle painterly brushwork, warm palette
  consistent with the game's overall art style
- Background: simple, low-detail garden/underbrush vignette — soft dirt and
  leaf-litter tones — no scenery, no other characters, no text

Critical:
- No text, numbers, icons, logos, or UI chrome anywhere in the image
- No hard outlines / no flat vector style — must match the painterly gouache
  texture of the board art
- 3:4 portrait aspect ratio, high resolution, print-quality, centered subject
  with even margin for card-frame cropping
```
